Your meetings, remembered.

Claudia is a meeting recorder and secretary for Windows. It records the meetings you start, transcribes them on your own PC, and turns them into notes, people and action items.

Records with consent

Starts only when you start it and asks you to confirm that everyone agreed. Your mic, the other side's audio and your screen.

Transcribes locally

Speech-to-text and speaker recognition run on your own graphics card. Recordings stay on your computer.

Notes and action items

A summary of every meeting, who said what, and the tasks that came out of it, with live captions while you talk.

Syncs your tasks

Optionally sends action items to Microsoft To Do or Google Tasks, into the lists you choose, and keeps them in step both ways.

Private by design. Claudia has no servers and collects nothing. Your meetings, notes and tasks live in a folder on your PC. Read the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Claudia is a desktop app for Windows that records the meetings you start, transcribes them on your computer, and keeps notes, people and action items for you. This policy explains what Claudia does with your data. In short: your data stays on your computer, the developer never receives it, and Claudia only contacts other services for features you turn on.

Where your data is kept

  • Recordings, transcripts, notes, voiceprints, people profiles and action items are stored in a data folder on your computer that you choose. They are not uploaded to the developer or to anyone else by Claudia.
  • The developer runs no servers for Claudia and receives no data from it. Claudia has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • You can delete any meeting, or the whole data folder, at any time. Uninstalling Claudia does not need any online step.

Recording and consent

Claudia never starts recording on its own. Every way of starting a recording asks you to confirm that everyone in the meeting agreed to be recorded. You are responsible for getting that consent under the laws that apply to you.

Services Claudia uses, and when

  • Anthropic (Claude). Meeting summaries, live hints, and questions you ask Claude about your meetings use Claude Code signed in with your own Claude account. When you use these features, the relevant transcript, notes or action items are sent to Anthropic under your account and Anthropic's terms. Claudia's developer has no access to them.
  • Hugging Face. Speech models are downloaded from Hugging Face, using your access token if you provide one. No meeting data is sent.
  • Microsoft To Do and Google Tasks. Only if you connect them, as described below.

Google user data

If you connect Google Tasks, Claudia asks for these permissions:

  • Google Tasks (create, edit, organise and delete your tasks);
  • your email address and OpenID (to show which Google account is connected).

How Claudia uses it

  • Reads the names of your task lists to find the lists you picked in Claudia, and creates a list if you named one that does not exist yet.
  • Adds your meeting action items as tasks in those lists. Each task holds the action item, its priority and due date, the meeting title and time, the owner when it is someone else's task, any notes, and a link to the meeting notes on your computer.
  • Updates, completes or deletes those tasks when you change, finish or remove the action item in Claudia.
  • Reads back whether the tasks Claudia created were completed or deleted in Google Tasks, so Claudia can mark them the same way.
  • Reads your email address only to display the connected account.

Claudia does not read, import or change any other task in your Google account.

Storage, sharing and deletion

  • Your Google sign-in is encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) and stored only on your computer. The links between action items and Google tasks, and their completed or open state, are stored in Claudia's local database.
  • Google user data is not sent to the developer, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train AI models. It is not shared with anyone, except that when you ask Claude about your action items, Claude Code may read their completed or open state from Claudia's local database under your own Claude account.
  • To disconnect, open Claudia's Settings, then Task sync, then Disconnect. Claudia deletes the stored sign-in and asks Google to revoke its access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Tasks already created stay in Google Tasks until you delete them there.

Claudia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft account data

If you connect Microsoft To Do, Claudia asks for the Tasks.ReadWrite permission and uses it the same way as for Google Tasks: it reads the names of your lists, adds and updates the tasks it creates (with an optional Outlook category), and reads back whether they were completed or deleted. It does not read or change your other tasks. Your Microsoft sign-in is encrypted with Windows Data Protection and stored only on your computer. Disconnecting in Claudia deletes it; you can also remove Claudia's access at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or through your organisation's administrator.

Children

Claudia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
